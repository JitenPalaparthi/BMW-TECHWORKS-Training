Acme Technologies Employee Handbook

Acme Technologies provides employees with 24 paid leave days every year.
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused leave days into the next year.
Employees receive health insurance coverage for themselves, their spouse, and up to two children.
Remote employees receive an annual home-office allowance of 30,000 rupees.
Employees receive a learning and development allowance of 50,000 rupees per year for approved technical courses, certifications, books, and conferences.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.
The normal working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM Monday through Friday.
Employees completing five years of continuous service receive an additional five days of paid leave per year.
